## Scope and remaining evidence

- The Nano is the documented focused baseline, not verified identity of the later owned UNO-format candidate. No electrical migration or final controller selection is made.
- A necessary PASS covers only the declared partial header reservations. Temperature interfaces, Pi link, final power and the complete controller assignment remain unresolved.
- Board labels, Arduino default pin numbers and raw ESP-IDF GPIO numbers are separate namespaces. Output GPIO_NUM tokens are reference facts, not generated firmware configuration.
- UART and strapping exclusions and default-bus-only routing are conservative V1 policies. The manufacturer GPIO matrix permits other routes that need a separate review.
- Independent pressure enable signals do not qualify a switch, polarity, reset-time state, pull network, drive/current, leakage, back-power or sequencing.
- The card-detect signal reserves a future custom-socket input; it does not establish a selected socket or a card-detect capability on Adafruit #4682.
- Optional IRQ reservations do not select an ADC topology, implement acquisition or prove a conversion schedule.
- No actual board interrogation, wiring, circuit assets, footprints, nets, EDA generation or physical tests are performed. All fourteen product contracts remain incomplete.
